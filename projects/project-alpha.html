About This Project

CMMI-001 is a longitudinal study investigating gut microbiome dynamics in patients with inflammatory bowel disease (IBD), including both Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis.

Stool samples are collected at enrolment and at 3-month intervals over two years. Paired metagenomics and metatranscriptomics capture both the structural composition and the active transcriptional output of the gut microbial community.

Primary aim: identify microbial taxa and functional pathways associated with IBD flares, remission, and response to biologic therapy.
