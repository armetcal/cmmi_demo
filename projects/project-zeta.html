About This Project

CMMI-003 investigates the gut-brain axis in Parkinson's disease (PD). PD patients and age/sex-matched healthy controls provide stool and plasma samples for multi-omics profiling.

Metagenomics and metatranscriptomics characterise the gut microbial community, while proteomics identifies disease-associated host and microbial proteins in plasma.

Primary aim: identify gut microbiome and proteomic signatures that distinguish early-stage PD from healthy controls and may serve as biomarkers.
