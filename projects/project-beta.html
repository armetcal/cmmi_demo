About This Project

CMMI-002 characterises the gut microbiome and plasma metabolome in a cohort of healthy older adults (65–85 years) to understand how the microbiome changes with healthy aging.

Paired stool and plasma samples are collected alongside detailed clinical metadata including diet, medication use, cognitive assessments, and frailty indices.

Primary aim: identify microbial and metabolic signatures associated with healthy vs. accelerated aging trajectories.
