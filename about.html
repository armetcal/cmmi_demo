Who We Are

The overall goal of the Canadian Microbiome Mapping Initiative (CMMI) project is to develop a multi ‘omics platform comprising state-of-the-art technology for ‘omics assessments and integrated data analysis to investigate the biological mechanisms and pathways by which the microbiome influences human health and disease. The project will further test, optimize and validate this platform for use in a clinical context, namely Parkinson’s Disease.

Our Research

CMMI consists of three cohorts, some of which are still to be collected:

  • Pilot cohort – method development and validation
  • Healthy Aging cohort – identify markers of healthy aging in older adults
  • Parkinson's Disease cohort – identifying putative disease mechanisms
