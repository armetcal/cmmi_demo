Who We Are

The Centre for Microbiome Medicine & Immunology (CMMI) is a multidisciplinary research group based at the University of British Columbia (UBC). Our mission is to understand how the gut microbiome shapes human health across the lifespan — from healthy aging to neurodegenerative disease.

We bring together expertise in microbiology, immunology, bioinformatics, epidemiology, and clinical medicine to conduct integrative, multi-omics research.

Our Research

The CMMI conducts longitudinal cohort studies that collect stool, blood, and urine samples from participants across three groups:

  • Pilot cohort – method development and validation
  • Healthy Aging cohort – tracking microbiome changes in older adults
  • Parkinson's Disease cohort – exploring the gut-brain axis

Each sample set undergoes one or more of the following analyses: metagenomics, metatranscriptomics, metabolomics, and proteomics. Clinical metadata are collected at each visit.
