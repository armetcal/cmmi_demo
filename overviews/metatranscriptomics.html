Raw Data Workflow

Purpose

To characterise the active transcriptome of the gut microbial community — i.e., which genes are being expressed at the time of sampling. Metatranscriptomics complements metagenomics by revealing functional activity rather than just genetic potential.

Data Types

Raw data type(s): FASTQ files (2×150 bp, stranded)

Processed data type(s): gene expression count tables, phyloseq objects (combined with metagenomics for paired analysis in R)

Data location: Currently stored on Sockeye / Compute Canada
/arc/project/cmmi/metatranscriptomics/

Key Considerations

RNA is inherently less stable than DNA — sample handling is critical. Paired metagenomics data are needed to normalise expression by gene copy number.
  • rRNA dominates: ribosomal RNA can account for >95% of total RNA; depletion is essential to enrich for mRNA.
  • Paired analysis: MTX/MGX ratios reveal which genes are being actively transcribed relative to their abundance in the genome.
  • Low-input challenge: stool RNA is often degraded; RIN scores should be checked before sequencing.

Recommended Tools

  • QC: fastp, FastQC
  • rRNA depletion check: SortMeRNA
  • Alignment: STAR (host), bowtie2 (microbial)
  • Quantification: featureCounts, HTSeq
  • Differential expression: DESeq2, edgeR
